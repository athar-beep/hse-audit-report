1  Executive Summary

A planned internal HSE audit of the steel fabrication and erection works was conducted over two days (10–11 November 2026) against ISO 45001:2018 criteria, supported by US OSHA 29 CFR 1926 benchmarks and UK HSE guidance. Twelve management-system elements were assessed through site walkdowns of the fabrication shop, pipe and structural erection fronts, laydown areas and welfare facilities, together with document review and interviews with supervision and workforce representatives.

The project achieved an overall compliance score of 76% — CONDITIONAL: improvement required. The management system is established and largely implemented: leadership commitment is visible, the lifting-gear register and plant pre-start inspection discipline are genuinely strong, and incident reporting is mature. However, the audit identified one critical finding — a suspended load passed over an occupied walkway without a barricaded exclusion zone — which required an immediate stop-work intervention during the walkdown, and three major nonconformities in permit-to-work closure, scaffold inspection and electrical safety. These are not paperwork gaps; each represents a control that had already failed in the field at the time of the audit.

Fourteen findings are recorded: 1 Critical, 3 Major Nonconformities, 5 Minor Nonconformities, 3 Observations and 2 Good Practices. Every finding carries a corrective action, an accountable owner and a target date. A follow-up verification audit is recommended within 30 days — a control is closed only when it is seen working in the field, not when a form is signed.

Auditor's note: Scores in this report follow one rule throughout — a control earns credit only where it was observed implemented and verified. A written procedure that nobody follows scores the same as no procedure.

2  Scope, Criteria & Methodology

Scope

  • Areas: fabrication shop (cutting, fit-up, welding bays), structural erection front, pipe spool installation area, laydown and material storage yards, temporary electrical installations, welfare and first-aid facilities.
  • Activities sampled: lifting operations, hot work, work at height and scaffolding, grinding and abrasive wheels, electrical distribution, material storage and housekeeping, emergency preparedness.
  • Out of scope: subcontractor head-office systems, design engineering, radiography source handling.

Audit criteria

  • ISO 45001:2018 — clauses 5 to 10.
  • US OSHA 29 CFR 1926 — Subparts C, L, M, P and CC benchmarks for field controls.
  • UK HSE published guidance on permit-to-work, electrical safety and workplace transport.
  • The project's own HSE Plan, task risk assessments and JSAs.

6  Conclusion & Follow-Up

This is a project with a working safety system and a specific problem: its two highest-energy activities — lifting and work at height — are exactly where field discipline is weakest. The paperwork, in most areas, is better than the practice. Closing that gap does not require new procedures; it requires the existing ones to be enforced at the workface, every shift, especially at shift change and under programme pressure — the two moments when F-01 and F-02 occurred.

  • Follow-up verification audit: within 30 days, covering F-01 to F-04 closure in the field.
  • Next full internal audit: next quarter, with lifting and scaffolding as mandatory deep-dive elements.
